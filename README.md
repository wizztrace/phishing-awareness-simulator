# Phishing Awareness Simulator

An interactive fake inbox that teaches people how to spot phishing emails. It was built for the **Wizztrace** awareness video.

**Try it live:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/phishing-awareness-simulator/

## What it is

A single web page that looks like an email client. It contains 8 emails: 7 phishing attempts and 1 genuine message.

| # | Email | Phishing type |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | Bank KYC alert | Account-block threat, credential theft |
| 2 | Courier redelivery fee | Small-fee scam with a shortened link |
| 3 | Shopping reward | Too-good-to-be-true offer, look-alike checkout domain |
| 4 | Streaming payment declined | Account-suspension pressure |
| 5 | "CEO" gift-card request | Impersonation with no link at all |
| 6 | Payment receipt | Callback phishing (phone-number scam) |
| 7 | Shipping documents | Malicious attachment |
| 8 | Order confirmation | Genuine email, for comparison |

## How to use it

1. Open the page and click an email.
2. Click anything that looks suspicious (sender, subject, links, attachments, wording). Correct clues are highlighted and explained.
3. Hover over buttons and links to see their real destination in the status bar, just like in a browser.
4. Use **Show hints** for help, **Reveal answers** to see everything, and **Reset** to start again.

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No installation, no server, no internet connection needed.

## Important notice

This project is for **education and awareness only**.

- Brand names and styling are imitated purely to make the training realistic. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by any company shown.
- Every link is non-functional, and the page collects no data.
- Domains and phone numbers in the sample emails are examples. Do not contact them.

## Quick rules to remember

1. Check the sender's real address, not just the display name.
2. Don't trust urgency or threats.
3. Hover before you click.
4. Never share OTPs or passwords by email, link or phone.
5. Verify unusual requests by contacting the person or company directly through an official channel.

## Report phishing

Forward suspicious emails to your IT team, or use your email provider's "Report phishing" option. In India, you can also report cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or by calling 1930.
